## Notes

1. **Obsolete prefabs ("obs")** — Old sail versions retained for save backward compatibility. New saves use non-obs versions.
2. **Region codes:** A=Aestrin, M=Medi, E=Emerald, L=Large/Lagoon, Am=Aestrin-modified, Em=Emerald-modified.
3. **Good→Item mapping:** `GoodToItemIndex()` adds 170 for goods > 30. Goods 0–30 share indices with base items.
4. **Sliced variants** (indices 342–369) — Generated by slicing knives. Each has `FoodState.slicePrefabIndex` pointing here.
5. **Sail size numbers** (0.85, 1.0, 1.45, etc.) — Sail area multipliers for wind force calculation.
6. **(N)** suffix — Believed to indicate "new" items added in later updates.
